# Damaged In Transit
## Scope
Applies when a customer reports a damaged item on delivery.
## Evidence
Ask for clear photos of the package and the item. Claims must be reported within 7 days of delivery.
## Resolution
If inventory is available, reship the item at no cost to the customer. If inventory is not available, propose a refund.
## Carrier claim
File a damage claim with the carrier using the photos and the delivery proof. Claims for items valued above INR 5000 also require supervisor approval before the customer is told the outcome.
