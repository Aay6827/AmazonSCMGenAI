# Delivery Failure - Address Issue
## Scope
Applies when a carrier reports an incorrect, incomplete or unreachable delivery address.
## Steps
1. Contact the customer on their registered phone or email to verify the address.
2. Update the address once only. A second address change requires supervisor approval.
3. Ask the carrier to reattempt delivery within 2 business days.
## Return to origin
After 3 failed delivery attempts the shipment is returned to origin. Refund is processed only after the return-to-origin scan is received, never before.
