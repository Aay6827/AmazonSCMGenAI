# Carrier Delivery SLAs
## ExpressOne
ExpressOne delivers metro lanes within 24 hours, non-metro lanes within 48 hours and remote lanes within 72 hours. Escalation goes to the ExpressOne tier-1 desk.
## RoadRunner Logistics
RoadRunner Logistics delivers metro lanes within 36 hours, non-metro lanes within 60 hours and remote lanes within 96 hours. Escalation goes to the RoadRunner regional manager.
## SwiftPost
SwiftPost delivers metro lanes within 48 hours, non-metro lanes within 72 hours and remote lanes within 120 hours. SwiftPost does not operate on public holidays, so those days are excluded from the SLA clock.
