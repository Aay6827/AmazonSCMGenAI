# Action Approval Thresholds
## Principle
No write action on an order or shipment is executed automatically. Every proposed action requires human approval at the level below.
## Refunds
Refunds up to INR 2000 can be approved by an L1 agent. Refunds from INR 2001 to INR 10000 need a team lead. Refunds above INR 10000 need a manager.
## Reships
Reships with a replacement cost up to INR 3000 can be approved by an L1 agent. Higher cost reships need a team lead.
## Carrier tickets
Carrier escalation and trace tickets can be raised by an L1 agent without further approval.
